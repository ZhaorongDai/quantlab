<objective>
Give a store whose roster has grown since it was created a forward path. Today a US-equity
incremental refresh HALTS on the first new listing: `XrBackend.append` →
`_assert_append_compatible` refuses a changed `symbol` coordinate, correctly, and there is no
tooling to get past it.

Two directions, BOTH implemented, direction 1 first:

1. **Widen in place** (Task 1, backend layer) — reindex the stored panel AND the incoming
   window onto the symbol union, NaN-backfilling history for the new listing. Keeps the store,
   keeps history, pays a full rewrite.
2. **Detect and rebuild** (Task 2, `Dataset` layer) — compare the incoming union against the
   store's and re-densify every window from raw. Simpler, obviously correct, recovers the new
   listing's REAL history, pays a full re-densify.

Purpose: any periodic refresh of a US-equity store hits this — new listings are routine.

Non-goal, stated so it is not drifted into: **`_assert_append_compatible` is not relaxed.** The
count-unchanged / labels-differ case (one delisting + one new listing) MUST keep raising from
plain `append()`; raw `to_zarr(mode="a")` succeeds silently there and re-attributes history
(measured 2026-09-06: `rows 0-4 were written for XYZ but are now labelled: ARM`). Both new paths
are separately-named opt-ins that SATISFY the guard rather than loosening it.

Output: a crash-safe backend widen primitive, a `Dataset`-layer strategy switch, a ledger
rebase, one CLI flag, and a test suite that pins the mis-attribution case specifically.
</objective>

   loudly instead of reading a half-widened store as authoritative.
