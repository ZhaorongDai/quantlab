### 7. Accept WR-01's checkpoint-vs-config policy, and decide on the spurious run_cv warning
expected: |
  You accept these three rules:

  - a checkpoint with no config.json beside it warns and continues.
  Known defect found in re-verification (no numbers affected): every normal run_cv fold logs a
  spurious "config.model says … using the checkpoint's dates" warning, 24 of them across 3 tests.

  compared as strings, so '2024-01-01' and a nanosecond string count as different.
  The proposed fix: skip the config.model comparison inside run_cv, and compare dates as timestamps.
