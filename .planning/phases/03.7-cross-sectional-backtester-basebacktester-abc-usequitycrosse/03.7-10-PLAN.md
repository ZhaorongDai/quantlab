<objective>
Deliver `run_cv()`, the model-CV backtest. It reads the training run's `cv_folds.json` (D-16, D-36), backtests each fold's out-of-sample test segment with that fold's own checkpoint and in-sample rule (D-17), and asserts the segments are contiguous before stitching. It produces both per-fold results and one continuous stitched out-of-sample curve (D-35).

Purpose: this is the user's "测试模型 cv 的实际交易能力" (test how well the model's CV actually trades). It only means something if every fold trades exactly its unseen segment, and if the stitched curve is a real single-capital path, not glued fragments.

Output: `run_cv` and its helpers in `quantlab/base/backtest.py`, `tests/test_backtest_run_cv.py`, and the D-02 lock extended.
</objective>
