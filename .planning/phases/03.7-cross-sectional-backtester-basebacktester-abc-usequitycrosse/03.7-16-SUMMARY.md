# Phase 03.7 Plan 16: G-03.7-8 DL predict_panel symbol-coordinate misalignment Summary

**DL `predict_panel` now selects the trained symbols in sorted order and re-sorts after the hook, so coords and values always come from the panel `to_array` lays out. `_save_model` records training symbols sorted. A position-sensitive MLP gets every prediction onto its own symbol for any record order.**

- `DLModel._align_prediction_symbols` keeps the WR-02 membership check exactly as it was: a missing symbol raises, and extras are dropped with a warning.
