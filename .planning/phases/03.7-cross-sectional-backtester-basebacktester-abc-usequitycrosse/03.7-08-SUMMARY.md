# Phase 03.7 Plan 08: In-Sample Detection and Sliced Metrics Summary

**`run()` now computes the model's effective training window in price-calendar bars. It warns when the backtest window overlaps that window and reports `whole`, `in_sample` and `out_of_sample` blocks, all cut from one vectorbt simulation. Slices use the engine's returns accessor on sliced `pf.returns()` plus order, trade and turnover records filtered to the slice. Turnover is defined as one-sided fill-bar notional over the previous bar's value. 11 tests lock this, and every one of 8 planted defects turned a lock red.**

## Accomplishments

- **D-17 window.**
  - `_label_horizon_bars` takes the maximum `n_forward_periods` over the model's labels. A label without the key warns, naming its class, and contributes 0.
  - `_training_window` searches the price calendar: the first bar on or after `train_start`, and the last bar on or before `train_end` plus the horizon in bars, clamped to the calendar end. With no train dates it returns `None` and warns.
  - `_split_window` returns `training_window`, `in_sample_range` and `out_of_sample_ranges` (0, 1 or 2 contiguous pieces). On a non-empty overlap it logs one warning naming both windows.
  - `run()` merges all three keys into the top level of `metrics`.
- **D-34 slices from one simulation.**
  - `_compute_metrics(simulation, benchmark, split)` returns `whole` (`_engine_stats` plus a `turnover` summary), `in_sample` and `out_of_sample`. Each slice merges `_period_returns_stats` with `_period_record_stats`, and is `None` when its range list is empty. `benchmark` appears only when a benchmark exists (D-08).
  - Nothing in the module calls `_simulate` a second time.
- **Engine.**
  - `VectorBtBacktester._simulate` fills `SimulationResult.trades` from `pf.trades.records_readable`, or an empty `xr.Dataset` when there are no trades.
  - `_period_returns_stats` slices `simulation.native.returns()` with `.loc[start:end]` per range, concatenates multiple pieces, and runs `.vbt.returns(freq, year_freq=MARKET.year_freq(...)).stats()`.
- **D-22 turnover.**
  - `_turnover` is a `timestamp` DataArray with one value per fill bar: sum of `|size| x price` divided by the previous bar's value (`init_cash` on the first bar). The Chinese docstring defines the measure.
  - `_turnover_summary` reports `mean_per_rebalance`, `sum` and `annualized` (mean x bars-per-year / `rebalance_periods`). With no fill bars, mean and annualized are NaN (persisted as `null`) and sum is 0.
- **Contracts lock.** `tests/test_backtest_contracts.py` now includes `_period_returns_stats` in the exact `BaseBacktester` abstract set. Its docstring says the engine implements four hooks. This is a deliberate update, not a loosening: `VectorBtBacktester`'s set is unchanged because the engine implements the new hook.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The two with visible consequences:

- The previous-bar turnover denominator follows the RESEARCH Code Examples recipe (`pf.value().shift(1)`). It keeps the fill bar's own P&L out of turnover.
- The comparison is day-normalized. For intraday bars, a training window ending on day D therefore counts every bar of day D as in-sample. That errs toward in-sample, which is the safe direction for D-17.
