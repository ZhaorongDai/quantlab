<objective>
Detect when a backtest window overlaps the model's effective training window (D-17). Report whole, in-sample and out-of-sample metrics from one continuous simulation (D-22, D-34), including a documented turnover measure.

Purpose: a backtest that silently scores bars the model trained on reports inflated skill. The vectorbt Portfolio cannot be time-sliced (RESEARCH Pitfall 5), so slice metrics must be assembled from sliced returns and filtered records, never from re-simulation, which would reset capital and change the path.

Output: date-window logic and slice metrics in `quantlab/base/backtest.py`, the engine's returns-stats hook and trade records in `quantlab/backtest/engine_vectorbt.py`, and `tests/test_backtest_metrics.py`. The abstract-method lock in `tests/test_backtest_contracts.py` is updated for the new hook.
</objective>
