<flagged_assumptions>
- **Importance types: weight, gain and total_gain.** One `get_score` call each, and they answer different
  questions: how often a factor splits, the average gain per split, and the total contribution. Summary keys are
  `importance_{type}/{factor}` so W&B groups them by the slash. For a multi-output Booster, xgboost aggregates
  importance across outputs; the doc says so.
- **Index mapping, not names.** `f{i}` -> `get_factor_names()[i]`. A key that does not parse as `f<int>` or whose
  index is out of range raises `ValueError`: it would mean the Booster was not trained by this `_fit_model`, and
  silently dropping it would misreport importance.
- **Written only when a recorder exists**, right after the existing best_iteration/best_score summary block.
  With early stopping, `self.model` is already the truncated Booster that gets saved, so importance describes
  the checkpoint.
</flagged_assumptions>
