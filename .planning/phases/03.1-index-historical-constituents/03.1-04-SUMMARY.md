## Registry Design

`UniverseCatalog.MEMBERSHIP_FETCHERS: tuple[type[IndexMembershipFetcher], ...] = (SP500MembershipFetcher, Nasdaq100MembershipFetcher)`.

Membership of this tuple is what gives a category its coverage boundary:

- `build()` calls `cls(cache_dir=...).build_intervals()` per member and tags each frame with `pl.lit(cls.CATEGORY)`. The `nasdaq_all` branch is untouched, the `how="vertical_relaxed"` concat and the final `.select([...])` are unchanged, and the table's schema and persistence are identical — only the number of category values changed, from two to three.
- `get_symbols_as_of()` builds `{cls.CATEGORY: cls.PIT_COVERAGE_START for cls in self.MEMBERSHIP_FETCHERS}` and raises when the requested category has a coverage start later than `as_of_date`. A category **absent** from the map keeps today's boundary-free behaviour.

**`NasdaqUniverseFetcher` is deliberately outside the registry.** It is a full-exchange roster from Tiingo's `supported_tickers.csv`: it has per-symbol listing dates but no index-membership concept and therefore no coverage start, and D-02 locks its `nasdaq_all` semantics exactly as they are. Registering it would impose a boundary it must not have. `test_nasdaq_all_has_no_coverage_boundary` is what makes that absence a tested property rather than an accident of the current tuple contents.

The `ValueError` message keeps its wording verbatim apart from interpolating the category and its coverage start — the left-censorship explanation and the "rather than silently defaulting to an incomplete/wrong answer" clause are the whole point of the guard, and they now render for every index rather than for one.

## Decisions Made

- **Two stores, never one.** The Nasdaq-100 panel gets `nasdaq100_constituent.zarr`. In a boolean panel a fabricated pre-coverage region is indistinguishable at read time from a genuine "nobody was a member", so a shared axis would silently assert 1976 NDX coverage. A consumer wanting both opens both and joins on the intersection of their timestamp axes — a deliberate, visible step.
- **Registry over `if`-branch.** The alternative (adding a second hardcoded category check) works today and fails silently at index three. Driving the boundary from each fetcher's own `PIT_COVERAGE_START` makes "a category with no boundary" impossible to create by omission.
- **`cache_dir` stays shared** across `sp500_constituent_config()`, `nasdaq100_constituent_config()` and `universe_config()`. Each fetcher writes its own `CACHE_FILENAME` inside it, so one directory holds one snapshot per index with no chance of two copies of one snapshot drifting apart.
