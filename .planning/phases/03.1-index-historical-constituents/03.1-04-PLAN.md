plan may modify any file under `base/`. It is measured against a SHA pinned before Task 1 does any
work, not against the working tree: GSD commits per task, so `git status --porcelain -- base/` alone

`_clamp_coverage_start`, not the `config` property. If you find yourself needing to change

drawn in 03.1-03 was wrong, and a per-index special case papered over in `dataset/` would hide it.

**`UniverseCatalog` currently hardcodes both its category list and its single coverage guard**
(`acquisition/universe.py:263-307`). `build()` names `NasdaqUniverseFetcher` and
`SP500MembershipFetcher` inline with two `pl.lit(...)` category literals; `get_symbols_as_of()`
guards `sp500_constituent` against `SP500MembershipFetcher.PIT_COVERAGE_START` in a hardcoded `if`.
Replace both with a class-level registry `MEMBERSHIP_FETCHERS: tuple[type[IndexMembershipFetcher], ...]`
holding the two index fetchers, and derive the category token and the coverage start from each
fetcher's own `CATEGORY`/`PIT_COVERAGE_START` constants. RESEARCH Finding 6 bullet 4's warning —
"two categories with different left edges must not be silently unioned" — applies exactly here: a
hardcoded guard that knows only one category means the second one silently answers pre-coverage
queries with an incomplete roster, which is the failure mode DATA-05 exists to prevent.
`NasdaqUniverseFetcher` stays outside the registry: it is a roster fetcher with a different shape
and no coverage boundary, and D-02 locks its semantics as-is.

Preserve the existing `ValueError` message's reasoning verbatim apart from interpolating the
category and the date — it explains that the change log is left-censored and that the query
"cannot be answered correctly, rather than silently defaulting to an incomplete/wrong answer",
which is the whole point of the guard.

**Two panels, two stores, two left edges.** The Nasdaq-100 panel gets its own
`nasdaq100_constituent.zarr`, never a shared axis with the S&P panel. A consumer that wants both
opens both and joins on the intersection of their timestamp axes; that is a deliberate, visible
step rather than an implicit union that would imply 1976 coverage for an index whose data starts in
2007.

`get_symbols_as_of` path must remain boundary-free, which the registry preserves because it is not
