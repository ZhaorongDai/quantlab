### Locked Decisions

**The axis**

- **D-01: The `symbol` axis dtype is `int64`.** Not a digit-string. PERMNO is an integer, and int64
  removes the ordering trap permanently. The repo's ~10 `sorted(str(symbol))` call sites are correct
  *today* only because historical PERMNOs happen to be 5 digits (≈10000–93436) — and nothing
  validates that (`scripts/ingest_wrds_crsp.py:296-304` and `base/config.py:377-380` only check
  `isdigit()`). `crsp_membership.py:326-345` is the one place that already handles numeric ordering
  correctly, and its docstring explains the failure mode. int64 makes the whole question disappear.
  - Accepted cost: ~40 `str(symbol)` coercions change, plus the five hard break points in D-09.
  - Accepted bonus: int64 coords also remove the existing zarr string-coord dtype round-trip problem
    documented at `dataset/backend.py:1020-1044`.

- **D-02: CRSP only. No cross-vendor identity work this round.** Tiingo, Alpaca and Binance keep
  their ticker axes. No `(ticker, date) → PERMNO` mapping layer. Binance can never have a PERMNO, and
  mapping Tiingo/Alpaca through CRSP would make non-CRSP vendors downstream dependents of CRSP and
  add an as-of-date correctness risk.
  - **Known consequence, accepted:** mixing a PERMNO-axis CRSP panel with a ticker-axis Tiingo factor
    produces a silent all-NaN result, not an error — `xr.combine_by_coords` (`base/model.py:207`,
    `:228`, `:237`) outer-joins into a union axis with an empty intersection, and
    `base/backtest.py:756-757`'s `predictions.reindex(symbol=prices.symbol.values)` then yields an
    empty-portfolio backtest with only a `selection.py:163` warning. A few-line dtype/intersection
    assertion at those two merge points would turn silence into a loud refusal. **Optional task —
    propose it, let the operator decide; do not assume it is wanted.**

- **D-03: `ticker` does not enter the panel.** It goes in a sidecar, queried as-of at display time,
  matching the existing `.crsp_filter_report.json` / `.crsp_adjustment.json` pattern.
  - A 2-D `ticker(timestamp, symbol)` string variable trips three existing guards:
    `dataset/backend.py:470-505` and `:984-1005` both reject a non-float variable on the symbol dim
    that is not named in `fill_values`, and `:1020-1044` documents the string dtype round-trip
    problem. `crsp.py:1220-1229` also casts every data variable to `Float64`.
  - A 1-D `ticker(symbol)` coord could only hold "the last ticker", losing FB→META
    (`crsp_symbology.py:5` notes PERMNO 13407 is stable while its ticker changed).
  - **Only six places need ticker restored** (verified exhaustively):
    `backtest/engine_vectorbt.py:297` (liquidation log), `liquidations.json`
    (`base/backtest.py:1798-1804` + `engine_vectorbt.py:291`), `base/model.py:1207`/`:1216`
    (missing/extra symbol lists), `dataset/masking.py:148-163` (`missing_symbols`, documented as
    never truncated), `acquisition/inspector.py:485-510` (`browse_zarr`), `utils/cli.py:129-133`
    (`--symbols` help text).
  - **`report.html` needs nothing** — `utils/backtest_report.py` contains only two occurrences of
    `symbol`, both plotly `marker={"symbol": "triangle-up"}`. It prints no ticker at all.

**Scope discipline**

- **D-04: No backward compatibility.** The project is pre-production. Delete config fields outright,
  write no config migration, rebuild on-disk stores. Historical backtest runs becoming unreplayable
  is accepted. (Verified mechanics: `utils/module.py:74`'s `load_dataset_from_config` is
  `cls(config_cls(**config))`, so a removed field makes an old `config.json` raise `TypeError`; and
  `load_backtester_from_config` (~`:188-199`) refuses a *missing* field by design, per WR-06.)
  Do not write tasks or risk entries about config migration or deprecated-field retention.

- **D-05: `universe_filter.py` — delete condition (a) ONLY. Keep (b) and (c).**
  - **(a) the 9 `NON_COMMON_TICKER_PATTERNS`** (`:158-221`, consumed at `:355-362`, `:408-420`,
    `:544-548`, `:608-614`) are 100% a Tiingo-era artifact — the entire justification is a measurement
    of Tiingo's 14,481 `us_all` tickers. CRSP's `security_filter` (`equity_common`, `crsp.py:202-215`)
    replaces them **more strongly, not equivalently**: CRSP's type vocabulary contains no
    warrant/right/preferred/test-code encodings at all, so 7 of the 9 regex targets cannot exist;
    the one real "unit" is `sharetype=UG`, already excluded; and CRSP additionally excludes what the
    regexes cannot — ADRs (`AD`), ETFs/CEFs, unknown-type rows — **per date**, with an audit report.
    The regexes are static, date-blind, unaudited, and the doc admits 284 of 2,310 five-letter hits
    had no corroboration.
  - **Bonus for deleting (a):** removing the symbol-axis column drop at `:608-614` makes LS-3's
    "the symbol axis is independent of the date window" trivially true (the mask becomes
    threshold-only and never drops a column), which also removes one
    `DLModel._align_prediction_symbols` risk. Take this bonus explicitly.
  - **(b) `min_price` and (c) `min_dollar_volume` STAY.** They are liquidity filters and CRSP has no
    equivalent — `FILTERABLE_COLUMNS` is deliberately closed to per-day TYPE columns only and
    `resolve_security_filter:294-302` raises on a price column. The operator chose to keep them
    because the graph-rewrite mechanism they ride on (`:86-123`, injecting `Div(v, mask)` into every
    `CrossSectionalOp` input) is the **only** "cross-sectional operators see pool members only"
    mechanism in the repo; `dataset/masking.py:UniverseMask` does index-membership alignment and is
    not a substitute.

- **D-06: `quantlab/dataset/cleaning.py` stays, in full. Do not touch it.** It is not a Tiingo patch.
  `flag_anomalies` is the only thing that surfaced the CR-01/CR-02 defect, and `anomaly_flag` is the
  repo's only boolean data variable — wired into `base/data.py:872`,
  `dataset/backend.py:339`/`:489`/`:1004`/`:1412`/`:1507` (bool→float64 widening guards that name it
  in their error text), `dataset/masking.py:167-177`, `dataset/nbbo.py:368-375`, and five test files.
  Three shipped stores contain the variable, and two 03.10 SUMMARYs write "12 Tiingo + 15 CRSP extras
  + anomaly_flag" into the drop-in contract.
  - Likewise **do not** delete `enums/data.py:TRADEABLE_TICKER_PATTERN`,
    `acquisition/universe.py`'s preferred-share/baby-bond patterns, or the volume guard. The first
    two are Tiingo *acquisition* infrastructure (deleting them ends Tiingo acquisition, and the
    pattern guard exists because a full-market job was once killed on `NXG-R-W`); the volume guard is
    a **download-size** pre-check unrelated to cleaning, and CRSP's own ingest uses it.

- **D-07: 03.10's WR-05 is settled here, by deletion.** The operator's original choice was option (a)
  (add a `rows_dropped_carrying_delisting` counter), then revised it to defer once the axis change was
  decided — adding a counter to `resolve_collisions` / `active_over_delisting` would be building onto
  code this phase deletes. Measured scale, for the record: 98 genuine occurrences over 1962–2025
  (96 tickers), discarded `delret` median +0.001, only 6 below −20% and 4 below −50%.

**Reversal of a Phase 03.10 locked decision**

- **D-08: This phase reverses 03.10's "the panel `symbol` dimension stays the ticker valid at each
  date; PERMNO is kept as a data variable".** That decision was sound on what was known then; it is
  reversed on evidence measured after 03.10 shipped:
  - 8,719 of 36,990 tickers (**23.6%**) have been used by ≥2 PERMNOs; 18,850 of 39,506 PERMNOs
    (**47.7%**) share a ticker with another security; the worst ticker (`EAGL`) was used by 10.
  - `resolve_collisions` detects contention with `group_by(["timestamp","symbol"])` — **same-day
    only**. A sequentially reused ticker (A delists, years later B takes it) has exactly one PERMNO
    every day, is never `crowded`, and is concatenated across two companies with no check, no report
    entry and no warning. In a 2000–2024 window that is **3,095 of 3,205** reused tickers (**96.6%**);
    even 2015–2024 has 748. The module's own docstring states the stake: "two companies' prices in one
    series … every return across the seam would be fabricated" (T-03.10-16). The guard implements that
    intent for 3.4% of the population. This is the `SEAM-01` finding from `03.10-17-SUMMARY.md`.
  - **Recording the reversal is itself a deliverable.** Do not edit 03.10's history; state the
    reversal in this phase's artifacts and in ROADMAP/`example/wrds_crsp.md` where the old claim is
    made.

**Specific findings the plan must account for**

- **D-09: five int64 hard-break points.** The first is a real bug independent of this phase:
  `XrBackend.widen_symbol_axis` (`dataset/backend.py:451` → `795`/`869`) coerces requested labels to
  `str`, so against an int64 stored axis `reindex` treats every label as new and **silently empties
  the store** — the superset guard at `:455-468` `str()`s both sides so it does not warn, and
  `os.replace` (`:543-545`) then makes the empty store authoritative while the original is
  `rmtree`d. No exception, no log. Then:
  `DLModel._align_prediction_symbols` (`base/model.py:1220`, `.sel` with `list[str]` read back from
  JSON → `KeyError`, with a misleading message because the membership check at `:1201-1203` passes);
  the densify/reindex chains (`base/data.py:326`→`:340`, `dataset/stock.py:506-516`→`:549`,
  `crsp.py:1372-1377`→`:1441-1450`, `base/data.py:824-866`); the polars `is_in([str,...])` sites; and
  `tests/conftest.py:1460-1536`, whose `SYMBOL_COORD_ENCODINGS` model raises `AssertionError` on an
  int64 dtype and parameterizes five test files.

- **D-10: "having a ticker" is currently an undeclared admission filter.** `label_rows`
  (`crsp_symbology.py:409-425`) **discards** every row no ticker interval covers. Measured on the full
  reference tier: 34,839 of 191,048 interval rows (18.2%) have a NULL ticker, touching 30,197 of
  40,518 PERMNOs (74.5%) — median NULL run is 1 day (mostly the delisting day, which the carry rule
  rescues) — and **1,012 PERMNOs (2.5%) never had any ticker at all**. Those 1,012 can never enter a
  ticker-axis panel; on a PERMNO axis they appear for the first time.
  **This is not dead code being removed — it is an implicit responsibility being surfaced.** The
  replacement must be an explicit, stated admission condition on `securitytype`/`sharetype`/
  `securityactiveflg`, **folded into the existing `security_filter` mechanism rather than a new one**,
  and it belongs in the same wave as the axis switch (W1), not later.

- **D-11: six traps — code that looks like adaptation but is not.** Full line numbers in the research
  doc; summarised so a plan cannot miss them:
  1. **Two distinct "delisting carry" mechanisms.** `crsp_symbology.py:382-404` (supplies a *ticker
     label* to the delisting row) → delete. `crsp.py:960-976` + the `_DELISTING_FLAG` at `crsp.py:228`
     (makes the delisting row *inherit the previous day's security-filter verdict*, because its type
     columns go NULL) → **MUST STAY**; it is D-10 anti-survivorship-bias. They are mentioned side by
     side in `crsp.py:930-934`, which is exactly how the second one gets deleted by accident.
  2. **`collision_universe` has two duties.** Collision tie-break (`crsp.py:690-692`,
     `crsp_symbology.py:455-534`, `:585-589`) dies. The explicit-roster-overrides-`security_filter`
     duty added by 03.10-14/GAP-C (`crsp.py:801-813`, `:872-874`, `:884-919`, `:754-781`) **stays**.
     The field cannot be deleted; it should be renamed (D-04 removed the round-trip cost), and
     `crsp.py:754-781`'s "it now has TWO readers" docstring must be rewritten, not copied.
  3. **Deleting `unlabelled` widens the panel** — see D-10. A behaviour change, not a deletion.
  4. **`_assert_unique_panel_keys` (`crsp.py:1487-1521`) is not pure adaptation.** Its risk is covered
     upstream on a PERMNO axis by `wrds_crsp.py:818-840`'s `(permno, dlycaldt)` assertion, but a cheap
     downstream backstop is worth keeping; only the message needs changing (`:1520` mentions
     `symbol_overrides`).
  5. **`config.symbols` and `config.permnos` semantics converge** (`crsp.py:1367-1371` vs `:481-483`;
     the comments at `crsp.py:159-160` and `config.py:112-113` insist on the distinction). On a PERMNO
     axis they filter the same thing. Needs an explicit decision — this one was NOT pre-decided.
  6. **`wrds_crsp.py:317-319` reads like ticker adaptation but is the reverse** — it observes that a
     PERMNO digit-string satisfies `TRADEABLE_TICKER_PATTERN` so the base path guard works unchanged.
     **Leave it alone.**

- **D-12: knock-on invalidation, accepted under D-04.** `ChunkLedger.fingerprint`
  (`base/chunking.py:203-211`) and the backtest fingerprint (`utils/fingerprint.py:53`, `:58`) are
  order-sensitive over the symbol axis, so both change and existing ledgers/runs are invalidated
  (`backtest.py:1145-1188` warns, does not block). Checkpoints' `trained_on.symbols`
  (`model.py:310`, `:507-514`) are `list[str]` — see D-09.

- **D-13: layers that are completely opaque to the symbol value** (no work needed, do not plan any):
  KunQuant (`base/factor.py:283-306` — it only receives a float32 `[time, symbol]` array and passes
  `symbols` back as an opaque ndarray; the sole constraint is that the axis length is a multiple of
  the SIMD block width, 8); tensor conversion (`model.py:266-282` — the value never enters the array,
  only `sortby` position); every factor/label definition; `backtest/selection.py:131-177`;
  `dataset/cleaning.py`'s dims-only checks; `get_xarray_dataset(["timestamp","symbol"])`; and the
  entire Nautilus path (`dataset/stock.py:573-583` is `raise ValueError("Not finished")` for US
  equities, so PERMNO has zero Nautilus impact).

## Summary

这个 phase 的本质是**删除一层中间适配**，不是新建管线：CRSP 的 raw tier 已经是 PERMNO 键
（`acquisition/wrds_crsp.py:317-320` 的 docstring 逐字写着 "Raw `symbol` is the PERMNO as a string, and
a typed `permno` Int64 column rides along."）`[VERIFIED: quantlab/acquisition/wrds_crsp.py:317-319]`，
ticker 化只发生在 `crsp.py:494-495` 两行。把这两行换掉之后，collision / class-suffix /
delisting-symbol-carry / PERMNO-seam / `symbol_overrides` 五套机器同时失去存在理由。

**本次调研把前置调研的两个悬空点实跑结清，并发现了三件它们没说的事。** 实跑结清的：
(1) **vectorbt 1.1.0 完全接受 int64 列索引**，`records_readable["Column"]` 回来是 object dtype 装 python int，
`.astype(str)` 得到 `'10107'`，与 `_signed_order_sizes` 的 `str(np.int64)` 完全一致（§R3）；
(2) **`XrBackend.widen_symbol_axis` 对 int64 轴确实静默清空 store**，superset 守卫一个都不报（§R2-A，附实跑输出）。
新发现的：(a) **破坏点是 7 个不是 5 个**——`dataset/masking.py:100-105` 和 `base/constituent.py:198/266/270`
会把已经对齐的 int 轴**强转回 str**，于是 `.sel` 抛 KeyError，`XrBackend.filter_by_symbol:1477-1479` 同理（§R2-F/G）；
(b) **trap 3「面板变宽」有硬日期边界**——1,012 个从无 ticker 的 PERMNO **全部在 1983-04-13 之前结束**，
carry 之后仍无法贴标的 4,015 个区间行最晚结束于 **1990-08-19**，所以任何起始日 ≥ 1990-08-20 的窗口
**变宽量为 0**（§R5c，这把一个设计难题降级成一个日期门）；
(c) **`securityactiveflg` 不在日度 raw tier 里**，D-10 点名的候选列用不了（§R5c）。

**W0 是可跑的，而且很便宜。** raw tier 与 reference tier 都在盘上（132 个 `.pqt` / 11 MB + 6 个参考表），
**离线重转不需要 WRDS 凭证**，sp500/2024 一次重转实测 **0.8 秒**。重转后
`anomaly_flag` **699 → 13**、`adjClose <= 0` **686 → 0**、`adjVolume` NaN **2202 → 1520**，
而 1520 恰好等于 `validate_schema` 报的结构性空格（稠密面板笛卡尔积）——**修复后的面板没有一个多余 NaN**（§R1）。

**Primary recommendation:** W0 用一个**类封装的离线重转入口**（不走 `quantlab/config` 工厂、直接构造
`CrspDatasetConfig`、调 `registry.convert`），先把 store 与 sidecar 重建并把 §R1 的四个数重测一遍写进 wave 产物；
W1 的 tracer 里把 §R2 的 7 个破坏点做成**先红后绿**的断言（尤其是 A 的静默清空，它是独立于本 phase 的真 bug），
并把 §R5c 的日期边界写成一条显式的准入条件而不是一段论证。

### A. `XrBackend.widen_symbol_axis` —— 静默清空整个 store（真 bug，与本 phase 独立）

机制三步，逐字引自源码：


`[VERIFIED: quantlab/dataset/backend.py:451, :456-459, :795, :869]`

superset 守卫**两侧都 `str()`** → `dropped` 恒为空 → 不报警；然后 `reindex` 在 int64 index 上找不到任何
一个字符串标签 → 全列变 NaN；最后
`os.replace(target, superseded); os.replace(widening, target); shutil.rmtree(superseded, ...)`

```
  [nan nan nan]
```

`[VERIFIED: measured 2026-09-20, uv run, xarray 2026.7.0 / zarr 3.3.0]`

### Pattern 1: 轴 dtype 归一化，而不是无条件 `str()`

**What:** 所有「把请求的标签对齐到存储轴」的地方，按 `stored[dim].dtype` 归一化请求，而不是一律 `str()`。
**When to use:** §R2 的 7 个破坏点全部是这一个模式的缺失。

```python
# 反模式（当前 backend.py:451，会静默清空 int64 store）
requested = [str(symbol) for symbol in symbols]

# 模式：按存储轴的 dtype 归一化，并让 superset 守卫在归一化后的值上比较
stored_index = stored[dim].to_index()
requested = list(pd.Index(symbols).astype(stored_index.dtype))
dropped = stored_index.difference(pd.Index(requested)).tolist()
if dropped:
    raise ValueError(...)   # 现在这个守卫才真的在守
```
`[Source: 本调研基于 quantlab/dataset/backend.py:451-468 的实跑结论构造；xarray 2026.7.0 语义]`

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **把「关掉一个开关」当成「删掉一个机制」。** `exclude_non_common=False` 在 CRSP 面板上是个
  仍会被 `to_dict()` 序列化、仍在文档里、但什么都不做的开关 —— 而 `is_common_ticker` 对数字串恒 True，
  所以它**看起来**在工作。D-05 要的是删。
- **只改 `_build_intervals` 就以为 W3 做完了。** `base/constituent.py:198/266/270` 会把它转回 str 轴（§R2-F）。
- **在 W1 换完轴之前不修 `widen_symbol_axis`。** 任何一次 `widen_and_append` 会无声抹掉 store（§R2-A）。
- **删 `crsp.py:952-977`。** 它和被删的符号学 carry 在同一段 docstring 里被并排提到（坑 a）。
- **用类型列去复刻「有没有 ticker」。** 测量证明那 1,003 个 PERMNO 的类型组合就是 `EQTY/COM/NS`（§R5c）。
- **把 `securityactiveflg` 加进 `FILTERABLE_COLUMNS`。** 日度 raw tier 里没有这列（§R5c）。

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: W1 换轴后第一次 append 抹掉整个 store
**What goes wrong:** `widen_symbol_axis` 无异常、无日志地把 store 换成全 NaN，并 `rmtree` 原库。
**Why it happens:** `:451` 无条件 `str()`，superset 守卫两侧也 `str()` 所以不报警（§R2-A，实跑确认）。
**How to avoid:** **W2 的这一条要提到 W1 之前或同一个 wave**；先写一个红测试（int64 store + 请求超集 → 断言
非 NaN 格数不变），再改 `:451`。
**Warning signs:** 转换「成功」但 store 变小、`adjClose` 全 NaN、`peak_window_bytes` 异常。

### Pitfall 2: W3 只改了 `_build_intervals`，轴还是字符串
**What goes wrong:** `UniverseMask.apply()` KeyError，或者更糟——两边都变成字典序字符串轴、"看起来对了"。
**Why it happens:** `base/constituent.py:198/266/270` 与 `masking.py:100-105` 各自 `str()`（§R2-F）。
**How to avoid:** W3 的验收条件写成「constituent panel 的 `symbol` coord dtype 是 int64 **且** 顺序是数值序」，
而不是「mask 能算出来」。
**Warning signs:** `UniverseMask.report()` 的 `missing_count` 突然变成全体。

### Pitfall 3: 误删 `crsp.py:952-977`
**What goes wrong:** 每只退市股的最后一天（带 delisting return 的那一天）被 security filter 丢掉，
幸存者偏差悄悄回来，而面板仍然完全良构。
**Why it happens:** 它和被删的符号学 carry 在 `crsp.py:929-934` 同一段 docstring 里并排出现（坑 a）。
**How to avoid:** W4 的任务描述里把这两段的行号并列写出来，并留一个断言：
「某只已知退市 PERMNO 的最后一行仍在面板里」。
**Warning signs:** `.crsp_filter_report.json` 的 `rows_dropped` 比 W0 基线多了大约「退市股只数」。

### Pitfall 4: 用类型列去复刻「有没有 ticker」
**What goes wrong:** 写出一条谓词，实测发现它要么一个都不排除、要么把普通股一起排掉。
**Why it happens:** 1,003/1,012 个 never-ticker PERMNO 的类型就是 `EQTY/COM/NS`（§R5c 实测）。
**How to avoid:** 改用日期门 + 计数断言（§R5c 的建议）。
**Warning signs:** 谓词写出来之后 `dropped_by_type` 里出现大量 `EQTY/COM/NS`。

### Pitfall 5: 按「832 个硬编码 ticker」估测试工作量
**What goes wrong:** 把机械替换和结构重写混在一起估，wave 规模失控。
**Why it happens:** 大量回测/模型测试用 `"A"`/`"S1"` 这类合成短名。
**How to avoid:** 按 §R9 的分诊表排；先做 `crsp_fixtures.py` 和 `conftest.py` 两个 chokepoint。

### Pitfall 6: 忘记 `test_widening_fixture_realism.py:238` 的字面量 pin
**What goes wrong:** 加了 int64 arm，这个文件红了，被当成回归。
**Why it happens:** 它逐字 `assert SYMBOL_COORD_ENCODINGS == ("fixed_width", "variable_length")`，
**存在的目的就是防止这个元组被悄悄改**。
**How to avoid:** 把它列进 W2 的显式改动清单。

### Pitfall 7: KunQuant 的 SIMD block width
**What goes wrong:** `kr.runGraph` 抛 `RuntimeError: Bad shape at open`。
**Why it happens:** 轴长必须是 8 的倍数（D-13 已记）。PERMNO 轴的**长度**可能与 ticker 轴不同
（§R1 实测 522→523，且 FB/META 合并会减 1）。
**How to avoid:** W1 之后跑一遍 `tests/test_factor_kunquant.py`。
**Warning signs:** 因子计算在换轴后才开始报形状错。

### Pitfall 8: `_write_identity_reports` 的 store-exists 守卫让 sidecar 停留在旧值
**What goes wrong:** store 重建了，sidecar 还是上一次的，审计文件描述一个不存在的面板。
**Why it happens:** `if Path(...).exists(): return`（`crsp.py:1414-1415`），docstring 逐字承认
"an APPEND no longer refreshes the reports"。
**How to avoid:** W0 的重建脚本必须**先删 store 再删四个 sidecar**（本调研的探针脚本就是这么做的）。

## Open Questions

1. **`config.symbols` vs `config.permnos` 的收敛方式**（CONTEXT 明说未预决）
   - What we know: `symbols` 是基类字段，有 12+ 个非 CRSP 读者，**不能删**（§R5e 实测枚举）；
     CRSP 里它只有两个读者（`crsp.py:1367-1371`、`:1445-1449`）；`permnos` 有 6 个读者且承载 WR-01 的空元组拒绝
   - What's unclear: 是否保留「按 ticker 选股」这个入口
   - Recommendation: **选项 A** —— 保留基类字段，`CrspStockDataset` config setter 拒绝非 None 的
     `config.symbols` 并指向 `permnos`，删掉 CRSP 里那两个读者。这同时把 §R2-G 的 KeyError 变成
     setter 期的清晰拒绝。**必须在 plan 里记成一条新 D-**

2. **`collision_universe` 改名成什么**
   - What we know: 字段必须留（职责 2），D-04 已消灭改名成本
   - Recommendation: `roster_universe`（与已有的报告 key `roster_overrides` 一致）

3. **D-10 的显式准入条件怎么写**
   - What we know: 类型列做不到（1,003/1,012 是 `EQTY/COM/NS`）；`securityactiveflg` 不在日度 raw tier；
     但**边界是日期**：一切相关区间最晚 1990-08-19 结束
   - Recommendation: 日期门 + 计数断言 + 报告字段，而不是类型谓词。**记成一条新 D-**

4. **`UniverseFilteredFactor.exclude_non_common` 的书面论证**（CONTEXT「Still open」第 3 条）
   - What we know: 本调研提供了「数字串对九条正则恒 True → 静默 no-op」的机制证据（§R7），

5. **`validate_schema` 是否给 CRSP 专属 `required_columns`**（CONTEXT「Still open」第 4 条）

| Pattern | STRIDE | Standard Mitigation | 本 phase 的处置 |
