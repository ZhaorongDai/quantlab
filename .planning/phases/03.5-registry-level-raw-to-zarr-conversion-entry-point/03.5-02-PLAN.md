<objective>
Split "ask" from "refuse" for the chunked RAM guard, and move the rendering of the answer out of
the acquisition layer.

Today `assert_chunked_panel_fits` raises INSIDE its per-chunk loop
(`quantlab/acquisition/universe.py:1912`), so a caller whose second window overflows never learns
what the fifth window costs — the chunk list is truncated at the first refusal. That is precisely
the case SC-4's "ask without starting" exists for. This plan adds a `estimate_chunked_panel` that
completes the loop and marks every over-budget window with its remedy, demotes
`assert_chunked_panel_fits` to the thin raising wrapper, hoists `_print_chunk_report` out of
`ingest_us_equity.py` into `quantlab/utils/cli.py`, and collapses the two-armed `--to-zarr` help
into one uniform flag now that there is only one conversion mode.

Purpose: DATA-08, and the `quantlab-console` requirement (`CVT-02`) that the guard and the
predicted peak be visible in a UI — which is why SC-4 is about ANSWERING rather than printing.

Output: `estimate_chunked_panel()`, `print_chunk_report()`, a one-mode `--to-zarr`, and
`tests/test_chunked_panel_estimate.py`.
</objective>
