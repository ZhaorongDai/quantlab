<objective>
Make the three US-equity shells consumers of the entry point rather than three independent
implementations of it.

Today each shell constructs `StockDataset` itself and calls a densify method directly:
`ingest_us_equity.py` the chunked one, `ingest_tiingo.py` and `ingest_alpaca.py` the whole-window
one. That divergence is what SC-6 is about, and it is also what keeps the programmatic path
unproven — an entry point with no in-repo caller is an entry point nobody has run.

With one conversion mode (D-06), delegation makes the `--chunk` / `--on-new-listing` divergence
disappear automatically rather than by growing new surface on the shells, and it switches the two
whole-window shells onto the per-chunk guard, which is the only guard left.

Purpose: DATA-07 and DATA-08 reaching real callers; SC-6.

Output: three shells that call `convert()`, two shells that gained the chunk flags, and two AST
test suites re-expressed so they keep pinning what they were written to pin.
</objective>

## Planner notes carried forward

- **The `symbols=None` probe feeding `refuse_conversion_without_raw_data` STAYS IN THE SHELLS.**
  The pattern map asked for this to be decided deliberately rather than drifted into. It is
  decided: that helper takes an `AcquisitionResult`, and `convert()` has none — `run()` is
  acquisition-only (03.4 D-14) and `convert()` is a separate call precisely so a conversion does
  not require an acquisition to have just happened. Moving the probe inside `convert()` would
  reintroduce that coupling in the one function built to remove it. All three shells keep their
  existing `refuse_conversion_without_raw_data(StockDataset(replace(ds_config, symbols=None)),
  result)` line, unchanged, immediately before the delegated call.
- **THE ALPACA MINUTE-SIZING QUESTION IS ANSWERED. DO NOT RE-OPEN IT.** An earlier draft of this
  plan told Task 2 to "decide deliberately" how `ingest_alpaca.py`'s minute frequency is sized under

  back to the developer and was **resolved on 2026-09-11 by amending D-10** — read the `AMENDED
  2026-09-11` block under D-10 in `03.5-CONTEXT.md`. `03.5-02` (Plan 02, Task 1) gives both chunked
  guard methods a keyword-only `bars_per_day: int = 1`, mirroring `estimate_dense_panel`, which
  already carries it. Task 2's job here is now mechanical: **carry the argument across**, unchanged,
  from the whole-window call to the chunked one. There is nothing left to decide and nothing to
  report as a finding.
  — **Reversibility of that widening: costly**, rated and carried in the owning plan
  (`03.5-02-PLAN.md` Task 1, which owns `quantlab/acquisition/universe.py`). Not re-derived here.
- **A test WILL go quiet if this plan is careless, and that is the point of Task 3.**
  `tests/test_volume_guard.py::test_every_entry_point_that_densifies_guards_the_dense_panels_ram`
  detects a densification by AST — an attribute call named `from_raw_data` or
