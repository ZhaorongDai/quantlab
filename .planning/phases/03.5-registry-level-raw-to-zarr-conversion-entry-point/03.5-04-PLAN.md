<objective>
Make the single entrance ticket to chunked conversion impossible to satisfy by accident.

`_raw_data_to_xr_window` is already the seam that turns chunking from a bounded WRITE into a
bounded DENSIFY. Its ABSENCE today is only a `logger.warning` (`quantlab/base/data.py:578-586`)
followed by silent degradation to whole-range densify plus slice. For onboarding a new data source
— the developer's stated reason for accepting chunked-only at all, "chunked-only 要方便接入新的数据"
— a warning is the worst possible shape: the newcomer sees their source *work*, and discovers the
missing method months later as an OOM at production roster size.

This plan makes it abstract on `MarketDataset` (not on `BaseDataset`, which keeps its concrete
default for the constituent panels), and gives `SpotKlineDataset` the explicit implementation that
keeps it instantiable with behaviour unchanged.

Purpose: DATA-07 — a conversion entry point whose one path is easy to join correctly and
impossible to join by accident.

Output: an abstract seam, a concrete spot override, two amended docstrings, and two updated pinned
assertions.
</objective>
