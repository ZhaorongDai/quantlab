<objective>
Gate the one new third-party dependency this phase needs (D-23: adding `exchange_calendars`
is a human-verify checkpoint), then build `XnysSessionCalendar`, the component that tells
the NBBO panel where each trading session starts and ends — including NYSE half days and
the DST switch.

Purpose: raw NBBO is collected 04:00-20:00 ET (D-04). A fixed 16:00 close on a 13:00
half day would turn after-hours quotes into in-session state — a plausible but wrong
panel. The calendar is the only thing that prevents it.

its tests. Plan 06 wires it into `NbboPanelDataset`.

plan 06 swaps this calendar in.

   - Docstring: why a calendar (half days would otherwise turn after-hours quotes into in-session state), that timestamps are naive UTC like every other timestamp in the codebase, that DST is handled by localizing per date, and that this module has no dependency on the acquisition layer.
