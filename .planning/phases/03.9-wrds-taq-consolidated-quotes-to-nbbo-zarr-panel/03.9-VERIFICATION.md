### Notes (non-blocking)

1. **Live interrupt-and-resume was not exercised.** 03.9-08's truth 3 has two clauses; the
   watermark-skip clause was measured live (re-run skipped every symbol), the "killed during day 2"
   clause was skipped because interrupting an orchestrator-run command is unreliable in this
   harness. I did not accept the SUMMARY's substitution on trust: `test_resume_per_batch_failure_resumes_at_the_failed_day`
   exercises the same invariant offline (passed), watermarks are written only after a successful
   fetch and go through `quantlab/utils/atomic.py:write_json_atomically` (temp + `os.replace`), and a
