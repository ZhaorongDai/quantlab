# Phase 03.9 Plan 02: XNYS Session Calendar Summary

`XnysSessionCalendar` gets every session's open and close from `exchange_calendars` (XNYS, starting 2003-01-01) and returns them as naive-UTC bounds. It handles DST and NYSE half days. The session window can be set anywhere in 04:00-20:00 ET. Edges inside regular hours are clipped to the exchange's actual open and close. Edges in extended hours are taken as wall-clock times and left unchanged.
