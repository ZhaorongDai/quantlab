<critical_finding>
**READ THIS BEFORE TOUCHING ANYTHING. It changes what the deletion can safely be.**

ROADMAP SC-3 deletes `estimate_dense_panel`. ROADMAP SC-4 declares the acquisition-volume
guard group UNTOUCHED. Those two are in direct tension, and the tension is REAL, not
theoretical. Verified by AST walk of `quantlab/acquisition/universe.py` on 2026-09-12:

```
assert_dense_panel_fits       -> estimate_dense_panel   (line 1808)   [being deleted]
estimate_chunked_panel        -> estimate_dense_panel   (line 1929)   [being deleted]
estimate_chunked_panel        -> plan_calendar          (line 1940)   [being deleted]
assert_chunked_panel_fits     -> estimate_chunked_panel (line 2022)   [being deleted]
estimate_acquisition_volume   -> estimate_dense_panel   (line 2184)   [SURVIVING per SC-4]
```

`estimate_acquisition_volume` — the heart of the group SC-4 protects — DEPENDS on the
estimator SC-3 deletes. It reads four keys off the returned dict: `symbols`,
`trading_days`, `observed_cells`, `density`. A literal reading of SC-3 breaks SC-4.

`quantlab/utils/cli.py:_explicit_symbol_catalog` makes it worse: its
`estimate_dense_panel` override is what lets `volume_pricing` price an explicit
`--symbols` list on a machine that has never built `universe.parquet`. Delete the
override outright and the `--symbols` volume-guard path falls through to the base
implementation, which needs a backend this synthetic view does not have.

**The resolution, and it is the whole shape of this plan.** `estimate_dense_panel` is two
things fused into one method:

- a **roster-window profile** — `symbols`, `trading_days`, `bars_per_day`, `timestamps`,
  `dense_cells`, `observed_cells`, `density`, derived from the catalog's own interval
  table clipped to the window and the 252/365.25 calendar. This is roster arithmetic. A
  roster catalogue legitimately owns it; the acquisition-volume guard genuinely needs it.
- a **dense-panel RAM estimate** — `dense_bytes`, `observed_bytes`, the `num_variables`
  and `bytes_per_value` parameters that produce them, and `MAX_DENSE_PANEL_BYTES` as the
  budget they are compared against. THIS is the capability SC-3 deletes and DATA-08 names.

Task 1 separates the two while changing no behaviour. Task 2 deletes the second and keeps
the first under a private name. The result satisfies both criteria honestly: the public
`estimate_dense_panel` symbol is gone (`not hasattr` passes), the dense-bytes capability
is gone, and `estimate_acquisition_volume` returns byte-identical numbers.

**Do NOT attempt to satisfy SC-3 by deleting `estimate_dense_panel` outright and inlining
its body into `estimate_acquisition_volume`.** That destroys the override seam
`_explicit_symbol_catalog` needs and silently kills `--symbols` pricing.
</critical_finding>
