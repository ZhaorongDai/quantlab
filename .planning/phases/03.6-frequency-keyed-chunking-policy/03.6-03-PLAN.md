<objective>
Make `TimeChunkPlanner` the SINGLE chunking authority by deleting `plan_calendar`, and
rewrite the two docstrings that describe the class as a two-planner design.

Purpose: `plan_calendar` existed to size windows BEFORE a download, when no timestamp axis
existed — and its only production consumer was `UniverseCatalog.estimate_chunked_panel`
(`universe.py:1940`), which plan 03.6-02 deleted. A sizing-only planner with nothing left
to size is dead weight carrying a documented footgun: its own docstring's first line warns
never to hand its windows to a densifier, and the only thing stopping that today is that
warning.

The docstring rewrite is not cosmetic. The class currently justifies `_period_key` and
`_group_by_period` being shared private helpers by arguing that drift between the two
planners is thereby structurally impossible. With one planner there is no such drift to
defend against — and the drift that DOES exist is the one plan 03.6-01 closed with a
trailing raise, between `GRANULARITIES` and `_period_key`. Leaving the old paragraph
standing would leave the class defending against a hazard it does not have while saying
nothing about the one it does.

Output: one planner, two rewritten docstrings, and a deletion lock.
</objective>
