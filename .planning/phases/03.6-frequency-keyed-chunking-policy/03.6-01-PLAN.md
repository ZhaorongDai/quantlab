<objective>
Extend `TimeChunkPlanner`'s granularity ladder from three rungs to five —
`("year", "quarter", "month", "day", "hour")` — and prove the new rungs reach all the
way to disk through the real chunked conversion path, not just through the planner in
isolation.

This is the TRACER slice for phase 03.6. It is the one piece of the phase that depends
on no deletion: it can land, be verified end to end, and be committed before anything is
removed from `UniverseCatalog`. If the ladder does not work, the phase has no reason to
delete anything.

Purpose: SC-1 is the phase's only ADDITIVE criterion. Landing it first means the
architecture claim ("a finer rung is a data point on an existing axis, not a new
mechanism") is proved on the executor's best early-context tokens, before three plans of
deletion work commit on top of it.

Output: a five-rung `GRANULARITIES`, two new explicit `_period_key` branches with a
trailing refusal, and tests that carry a `day` conversion end to end to a Zarr store and
an `hour` window across a real minute axis.
</objective>

<threat_model>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| operator CLI -> `--chunk` value | An operator-supplied granularity token crosses into `TimeChunkPlanner`. It is constrained by argparse `choices` derived from `GRANULARITIES` AND re-validated by `TimeChunkPlanner.__init__`, so a programmatic caller bypassing argparse is still refused. |
| raw parquet tier -> densify loop | Untrusted-in-size (not untrusted-in-origin) vendor data crosses into an in-memory dense panel. Window size is what bounds the allocation. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03.6-01 | Denial of Service | `TimeChunkPlanner._period_key` / `_group_by_period` at the `hour` rung | low | mitigate | A finer rung STRICTLY REDUCES peak RAM per window — `hour` is the safest rung on the ladder, not the riskiest.
