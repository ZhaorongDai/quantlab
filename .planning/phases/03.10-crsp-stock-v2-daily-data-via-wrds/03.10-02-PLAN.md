<objective>

anything is built sideways:
- a fake WRDS session serves Apple's (PERMNO 14593) real `crsp_a_stock.dsf_v2` rows around the 2020-08-31 4:1 split;
- a new CRSP provider lands them as raw daily shards keyed by PERMNO;

- a new `CrspStockDataset` turns the shards into a drop-in `[timestamp, symbol]` panel whose total-return `adjClose` is checked by hand.

It builds on plan 01, which already settled the structural questions:
- the neutral `quantlab/acquisition/wrds.py` descriptor module;

conversion time, so renames never touch watermarks or resume state. This is the identity
decision of the assumption-delta record in plan 01, and this plan implements it.
