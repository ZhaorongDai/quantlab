<objective>
Turn CRSP's PERMNO-keyed security-info history into the period-correct ticker the panel
uses as its `symbol` (D-04): renames, share classes (`BRK.B`), NULL tickers on delisting
days, fixed overrides (QQQ), and a deterministic rule set for two PERMNOs claiming one
ticker on one day — resolved or refused, never merged.

**Sequencing:** execute only after phase 03.9 is fully merged and plan 03.10-01 is merged.
Pure module; no WRDS, no disk beyond test fixtures. Plans 07 (price panel) and 08
(membership panels) consume it, so both panels label a PERMNO identically.

Purpose: the price panel and the constituent universes must agree on every symbol,
otherwise a universe mask silently misses its prices.

Output: the full `quantlab/dataset/crsp_symbology.py` and `tests/test_crsp_symbology.py`.
</objective>
