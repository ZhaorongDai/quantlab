# Phase 03.10 Plan 07: CRSP Point-in-Time Universes Summary

**The two universes this vendor actually offers now exist as PERMNO-keyed, point-in-time intervals with explicit, coverage-bounded ends: CRSP's own S&P 500 membership from `dsp500list_v2`, and the Compustat Nasdaq-100 history linked to PERMNOs through CCM on `gvkey` AND `iid = liid` — which refuses to return a universe at all while any membership day lacks a PERMNO.**

## Accomplishments

- **`quantlab/dataset/crsp_membership.py`** — `CrspMembership` over a `CrspReference`, with both universes behind one `permno_intervals(index, *, allow_unlinked=False)` returning `(permno Int64, start_date Date, end_date Date)` sorted by `(permno, start)`, plus `permnos_in_range()` as the CLI's roster query and a per-call `report`.
- **Every end is explicit and bounded by CRSP coverage.** A NULL end or an end past `CrspReference.product_end` becomes the product end and is counted in `report["clipped_to_product_end"]`; a spell that starts after it is dropped and counted in `report["dropped_after_product_end"]`. This is the whole reason the module exists in this shape: `IndexConstituentDataset._densify` sets an open interval's right edge to `max(observed, today)` — wall-clock today — so one null end would extend a CRSP universe months past the price coverage it is supposed to be bounded by.
- **The Nasdaq-100 join is security-exact.** `idxcst_his` at `gvkeyx = '000208'` joins `ccmxpf_lnkhist` on `gvkey` AND `iid = liid` with `linktype in (LC, LU, LS)`, and each membership spell is intersected with each matched link's own date range. The verbatim Alphabet rows (live keys `L8_2` and `L7_4`) produce exactly `(90319, 2005-12-21, 2025-12-31)` and `(14542, 2014-04-03, 2025-12-31)`; the three `NR` link rows contribute nothing.
- **The unlinked days are computed, not inferred.** After intersection the module subtracts the covered pieces from the clipped spell and classifies what is left: a stretch of at most `LINK_GAP_TOLERANCE_DAYS` (4) between two links is a seam in the link table and goes to `report["tolerated_gaps"]`; anything longer — and any spell with no link at all — is unlinked and raises by default, listing up to 20 spells with their uncovered ranges and naming `allow_unlinked=True` (the CLI's `--allow-unlinked-ndx`). With the opt-in the same records land in `report["unlinked"]` behind a loguru warning. That is T-03.10-23: a silently dropped spell is survivorship bias that reads downstream as a data gap rather than an error.
- **Two refusals guard identity.** One `(gvkey, iid)` mapping to two different PERMNOs on overlapping dates raises naming both PERMNOs and the overlap (T-03.10-25); a non-integral `lpermno` raises rather than being rounded into a different, real security (`lpermno` is `double precision` on the server, live key `L7_2`).
- **`tests/test_crsp_membership.py`** — 18 offline tests. Every fixture row is either verbatim from a named live-check key (`DSP500_ROWS`, `IDXCST_ROWS`, `CCM_ROWS` from `tests/crsp_fixtures.py`, which was not edited) or carries a `# SYNTHETIC` comment on the spot. ## Decisions Made

Recorded in the frontmatter `key-decisions`. The three a later plan will meet:

- **`iid = liid`, never `linkprim`.** The standard tidyfinance recipe filters `linkprim IN ('P','C')`. Applied here it keeps GOOGL and drops GOOG — one of the two Alphabet classes that are both genuine Nasdaq-100 members under one gvkey. The security-exact join is the only one that survives a multi-class issuer.
- **No link at all is never a "tolerated gap".** The 4-day tolerance exists to bridge a seam BETWEEN two links of one security. Applying it to a spell with zero links would let any membership of four days or fewer vanish without a word — the exact silent drop the tolerance was never meant to authorise.
- **Out-of-coverage is not unlinkable.** A spell starting after the product end (10 of them exist live) is dropped and counted; it does not join the unlinked list, so a user is never asked for `--allow-unlinked-ndx` merely because the CRSP annual product boundary exists.
