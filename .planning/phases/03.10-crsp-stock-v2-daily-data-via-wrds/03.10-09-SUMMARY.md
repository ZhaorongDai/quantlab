# Phase 03.10 Plan 09: CRSP-vendor universes as symbol-level membership panels Summary

**`CrspMembership.symbol_intervals` plus `CrspSP500ConstituentDataset` and `CompustatNasdaq100ConstituentDataset` — the two CRSP-vendor universes as ordinary `is_member` Zarr panels whose tickers are proved, end to end, to be the CRSP price panel's own.**

## Accomplishments

- **Symbol-level membership over the shared symbology.** `CrspMembership.symbol_intervals(index, allow_unlinked=False)` intersects each PERMNO membership window with `CrspSymbology(...).symbol_intervals()` — the *same* rule `CrspStockDataset._derivation` labels `dsf_v2` rows with — and returns `(symbol, start_date, end_date)`, exactly `IndexConstituentDataset._densify`'s input contract. Renames (FB → META) and share classes (BRK.B) therefore appear in the mask spelled the way the panel spells them.
- **A ticker handed between PERMNOs stays one membership.** Alphabet's `GOOG` passed from PERMNO 90319 to 14542 on 2014-04-03 with no day between; the per-symbol merge makes that one continuous `GOOG` membership, while the *price* panel still marks the handover through its `permno` variable (D-18).
- **Unlabelled membership is loud.** Membership days no CRSP ticker covers are dropped — they have no column to live in — but recorded per uncovered range in `report["unlabelled_members"]` with a `loguru` warning, so "no ticker" is never silently reported as "not a member".
- **Two more constituent datasets, at the cost of two methods each.** `CrspSP500ConstituentDataset` (coverage from 1925-12-31, fifty years earlier than the Wikipedia S&P panel) and `CompustatNasdaq100ConstituentDataset` (left-censored at 1995-01-01, twelve years earlier than the Wikipedia Nasdaq-100 panel, with GOOG *and* GOOGL as members) implement exactly `_pit_coverage_start()` and `_build_intervals()` — DATA-06's structural claim, held.
- **The agreement is proved by construction.** `test_membership_symbols_agree_with_the_crsp_price_panel` runs a real CRSP pull into the raw tier, a real `registry.convert(..., data_type="crsp_daily")` into a Zarr panel, and then resolves membership from the *same* reference directory. The two sides are compared as sets of `(date, symbol)` pairs per PERMNO, so a rename handled on one side only — the silent empty-mask failure this plan exists to prevent — fails the test.

## Decisions Made

- **No `overrides` into the symbology.** `symbol_overrides` (D-15) pins one PERMNO to one symbol for one *conversion*; a universe is a fact about an index, not about a store. An override only ever collapses a rename, so a caller who pinned `QQQ` in their panel still meets the panel's `QQQ` column either way.
- **Merge per symbol, not per PERMNO.** Two adjacent `GOOG` rows in the mask would be indistinguishable from a one-day gap in membership.
- **Unlabelled days are reported, not refused** — unlike an unlinked Nasdaq-100 spell, which *has no security at all* and still raises. These days have a real security CRSP simply never named, and a symbol-keyed panel has no column for them.
- **Two flat classes, no shared `_CrspVendorConstituentDataset` base.** An intermediate base was written and then removed: it moved the two hooks off the concrete classes, which is precisely what DATA-06's structural test forbids. Six lines of duplication is the correct price.
- **The right edge is asserted against a literal, never the clock.** `_densify` extends an open interval to wall-clock today, so a test reading `pd.Timestamp.today()` could not distinguish a correct panel from a look-ahead one (T-03.10-30).
