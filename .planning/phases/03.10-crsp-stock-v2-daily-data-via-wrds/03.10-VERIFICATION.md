## Verdict, stated plainly

**The architecture is sound and the pipeline is real. The panel is not drop-in.** Both are true,
and neither cancels the other.

What was actually built is substantial and correct at the seams: a per-capability vendor resolver
that lets one `wrds` descriptor serve two products without an import cycle; a PERMNO-keyed raw tier
with annual-vintage stamping, entitlement ordering and a page contract that refuses rather than
de-duplicates; one shared symbology that the price panel and both membership panels read, so a
rename never desynchronises them; a global adjustment anchor with a sidecar that refuses a
mismatched append; three provenance sidecars per store; and a CLI that opened exactly one live WRDS
session and pulled real data end to end. 179 CRSP tests pass, the full-suite regression gate is
clean against the 03.9 baseline (zero new failures, run twice in the main tree), and the operator's
seven-command live smoke hit its numbers exactly — AAPL 253 rows in 2020, the 4:1 split ratio
1.033912 exact, the FB→META seam on the right day with no overlap, a 520-PERMNO S&P roster with
503 members/day, QQQ close 614.31 exact, the 2026 request refused before any pull.

But the goal word is **drop-in**, and drop-in is a claim about what a consumer gets when it reads
the panel without knowing it changed vendor. On the store this phase shipped, four of 522 symbols —
CTLT, MRO, PXD, WRK, every one a real 2024 S&P 500 removal — have their **entire** `adjClose`,
`adjOpen`, `adjHigh`, `adjLow` history written as identically 0.0 and their **entire** `adjVolume`
history written as NaN. I re-confirmed this read-only against
`data/data/us_equity/1d/wrds_crsp_sp500_1d.zarr` rather than taking the review's word for it.
`alpha158.py` and `fret.py` read those five columns and nothing else: `Return` is
`Input("adjClose")`, so for those securities every label is `0/0`. The panel does not raise — it
produces NaN returns, `inf` ratios and a permanent bottom rank on every price-level factor, in a
well-formed store with green sidecars.

The securities destroyed are precisely the ones the survivorship-bias design exists to retain.
That is the phase's own stated reason for being: `example/wrds_crsp.md` opens its rationale with
「缺了退市收益 = 幸存者偏差」 and explains the delisting-carry rules at length. Those rules work —
the delisting return IS in `ret`, exactly once, on the right row. It is the adjusted columns, the
ones every consumer actually reads, that lose it. Bias removed at the front door and readmitted at
the back.

A further seven historical constituents (Blackstone 2007-2019, KKR 2010-2018, Carnival 2003-on,
Royal Dutch Petroleum entirely) are truncated mid-history by the default filter, and a truncated
series is downstream-indistinguishable from a late IPO.

The defects are **localized** — two of them are five lines in one function of one file, and the
fix leaves the total-return chain untouched (only the level it anchors to changes). The
architecture does not need rework. But "one file, five lines" describes the repair, not the impact:
until it lands, a research run over this panel is quietly wrong in the direction that flatters it.
**Do not proceed to a phase that consumes this panel.**

## Gaps Summary

Five gaps, three of them data-correctness, one CLI, one accounting.

**GAP-0 (blocker, close FIRST).** `tests/crsp_fixtures.py` has no `dlyprcflg='DA'` row, no zero
`dlyprc`, no NULL `dlycumfacshr`. `grep` for `'DA'` across `tests/` and `quantlab/` returns
nothing. The corpus encodes Lehman-2008's `DP` shape, which is 0 of 5 delisting rows in the pulled
tier; the shape that is 5 of 5 is untested. Until a `DA` fixture exists, GAP-A and GAP-B cannot be
held red and a fix cannot be proved.

**GAP-A (blocker).** `crsp.py:419` turns CRSP's `dlyprc = 0.0` no-price sentinel into a real
$0.00 close; `:429` admits it as the adjustment anchor because it tests `is_not_null()` and `0.0`
is not null. Result: `_close_anchor = 0.0` and the security's entire adjusted price history is
identically 0.0. Fix: treat `DA`/zero as no price, require `close > 0.0` for the anchor, and refuse
loudly when no anchor qualifies.

**GAP-B (blocker, independent mechanism).** The same row has NULL `dlycumfacshr`, so
`_cumfacshr_anchor` is null and the entire `adjVolume` history is NaN. Fixing `close` alone does
not fix this. Fix: require `dlycumfacshr.is_not_null()` on the anchor row too, and refuse when
nothing qualifies.

Together GAP-A and GAP-B destroy 4 of 522 symbols in the shipped 2024 store — CTLT, MRO, PXD, WRK,
all real S&P 500 removals — across both the price and volume adjusted columns, the only columns
`alpha158` and `fret` read. The ratio scales with window length, not with rarity.

**GAP-C (high).** `equity_common` drops `UG`/`AD` rows of real index constituents mid-history
(Blackstone 2007-2019, KKR 2010-2018, Carnival 2003-on, Royal Dutch Petroleum entirely; 7 of 1,956
historical S&P members). The operator has already decided the direction: an explicit roster
(`--universe` membership spell, or `--permnos`) must override the filter, reportably.

**GAP-D (medium).** `--qqq` with no other roster flag runs the equity conversion with an empty
roster, which `crsp.py:399` reads as "every PERMNO", collides with the stale `custom` store and
raises before the QQQ block runs. No `wrds_crsp_qqq_1d.zarr` exists. Fix the skip AND the
`None`-vs-`()` truthiness landmine under it.

**GAP-E (warning, docs only).** ROADMAP 03.10 still reads `**Requirements**: TBD` and no
REQUIREMENTS.md row mentions the phase, even though DATA-03 and DATA-05 are materially advanced by
it. The `D-xx` handling itself was correct and well documented — this is the unclosed other end.

Closure order: **GAP-0 → GAP-A + GAP-B (one plan, one file, fold in WR-01/WR-02/WR-03 while there)
→ GAP-C (design change across the filter/roster boundary) → GAP-D → GAP-E.** GAP-A/GAP-B also
require `example/wrds_crsp.md`'s delisting section to be corrected, since it currently documents a
guarantee the adjusted columns do not keep.
