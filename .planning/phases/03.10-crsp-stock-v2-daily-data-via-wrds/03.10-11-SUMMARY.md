### GAP-1 — data correctness, **HIGH** severity: `equity_common` silently truncates the history of real index constituents

**What is wrong.** The D-17 extended reading implemented in plan 08 —
`securitytype='EQTY'` AND `securitysubtype='COM'` AND `sharetype ∈ (NS, SB, CE)` —
excludes `AD` and `UG`. In CRSP, `UG` marks the **publicly traded partnership (LP) era** of a
security, and those eras belong to major index constituents. `AD` is not merely "a foreign ADR".

**The damage is NOT "the security is missing" — it is MID-SECURITY TRUNCATION.** Evidence
gathered from the live `_reference/stksecurityinfohist.parquet`: of the **1,956** historical
S&P 500 member PERMNOs, **7 are hit**:

| PERMNO | Name | Dropped range |
|---|---|---|
| 92108 | Blackstone (`BLACKSTONE GROUP LP`) | 2007-06-22 … 2019-06-30 — **12 years gone**, the series appears to start 2019-07 |
| 11990 | KKR (`K K R & CO LP`) | 2010-07-15 … 2018-07-01 — **8 years gone** |
| 75154 | Carnival (CCL) | 2003-04-21 … 2025-12-31 — **everything from 2003 onward gone**; this is the 252 rows dropped in the 2024 S&P run |
| 75592 | Plum Creek Timber | 1989-06-02 … 1999-01-03 |
| 14617 | Ares Management | 2014-05-02 … 2018-03-01 |
| 75241 | Pioneer / Parker & Parsley | 1987-12-22 … 1991-02-19 |
| 25267 | Royal Dutch Petroleum | `AD` for its **entire life 1954–2005** — the whole security vanishes despite decades of S&P 500 membership |

A truncated series is **indistinguishable downstream from a late IPO**, so this silently poisons
any factor or backtest over those names.

**Population context** from the same table (EQTY/COM rows by sharetype):
`NS` 127,317 rows / 30,263 permnos; `AD` 6,696 / 1,379; `UG` 1,508 / 394; `SB` 1,134 / 298; `CE` 43 / 18.

**USER DECISION (given explicitly by the human this session — this is the resolution direction; it is NOT implemented here):**

> 「优先保证成分股不缺」 — index constituents must never be missing.

The agreed principle: **the security filter screens an unspecified population; it must not
overrule an explicit roster.** Concretely:

- A `--universe` run: the index provider already decided membership → **a member is never dropped by the filter during its membership spell.**
- A `--permnos` run: the user named the securities → **not dropped.**
- A broad screen with **no** explicit roster: the filter applies as today, and excluding `AD`/`UG` remains meaningful there.
- The override must be **REPORTABLE**: `crsp_filter_report.json` records how many rows and which PERMNOs were kept by the override, so nothing is silent.

This **supersedes** the "reversible by passing the literal two-column dict" note in
`03.10-08-SUMMARY.md` as the chosen direction. That dict remains a valid escape hatch, but it is
not the fix. The fix itself belongs to a gap-closure plan touching `quantlab/dataset/crsp.py`
(and wherever the roster reaches the filter).

**Interim workaround for users** (already in the doc): pass the literal two-column predicate
`{"securitytype": ["EQTY"], "securitysubtype": ["COM"]}` — or `"none"` — as
`CrspDatasetConfig.security_filter`, and read `crsp_filter_report.json` to check what changed.

### GAP-2 — CLI defect, **MEDIUM** severity: `--qqq` alone crashes the Zarr conversion

With `--qqq` and no `--permnos`/`--universe`, the equity roster is empty but
`scripts/ingest_wrds_crsp.py` still calls the equity-panel `convert()` (printing
`Converting 0 PERMNO(s)`). It resolves to the `custom` store name, collides with a pre-existing
`wrds_crsp_custom_1d.zarr` built under a different adjustment anchor (`end_date` 2023-12-31 from
command 3), and `_assert_anchor_unchanged` raises `ValueError`. Exit 1 — **the QQQ benchmark store
is never written**, even though the QQQ raw pull succeeded and its data is correct (614.31 verified).

**The anchor guard behaved correctly.** The defect is in the CLI: it should skip the equity
conversion when the equity roster is empty. Fix belongs to `scripts/ingest_wrds_crsp.py`.

**Interim workaround:** run `--qqq` together with a real `--permnos`/`--universe`, or delete the
stale `wrds_crsp_custom_1d.zarr` and its three `.crsp_*.json` sidecars first.

### OBSERVATION (not a defect)

Command 5's unlinked-spell refusal evaluates the **whole Nasdaq-100 membership history** rather
than the requested window — all 6 refused spells lie in **1999–2008**, far outside the requested
2024 window, so a window-scoped check would not have refused at all. Conservative by design, but
it makes a 2024-only user read a 1999 gap report. Narrowing it to the requested window is optional.

### FOLLOW-UP (low priority)

`DEFAULT_BYTES_PER_ROW = 150` measured at **78.3** at S&P 500 scale. Conservative, so **not a bug**;
tightening it is optional and belongs to whoever owns `scripts/ingest_wrds_crsp.py`.
