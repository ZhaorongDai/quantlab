<objective>
**GAP-A and GAP-B — one plan, one file, because they are two mechanisms on the same five lines.**
Turn the four RED tests from plan 03.10-12 green, and correct the document that currently promises
a guarantee those columns do not keep.

`quantlab/dataset/crsp.py:419` computes `close = abs(dlyprc)` with no handling of CRSP's no-price
sentinel, so a delisting row's `dlyprc = 0.0` becomes a real $0.00 close. The anchor selector at
`:429` filters on `is_not_null()` alone, and `0.0` is not null, so that row BECOMES the anchor:
`_close_anchor = 0.0` makes the security's entire adjusted price history identically 0.0. The same
row has a NULL `dlycumfacshr`, read off the same anchor row at `:434`, so `_volume_factor` is null
and `adjVolume` is NaN for that security's whole history — an independent mechanism that fixing
`close` alone does not touch.

On the store this phase already shipped, that destroys 4 of 522 symbols — CTLT, MRO, PXD, WRK,
every one a real 2024 S&P 500 removal — across `adjOpen/adjHigh/adjLow/adjClose/adjVolume`, which
are the only columns `factor/alpha158.py` and `label/fret.py` read. Bias removed at the front door
and readmitted at the back.

**The total-return chain is not changed by this plan.** The delisting return stays in `ret` and in
the cumulative product, exactly once, on the right row. Only the LEVEL that chain is anchored to
moves, from 0.0 to the last real close.

Purpose: until this lands, a research run over a CRSP panel is quietly wrong in the direction that
flatters it, and every downstream phase would consume it.

Output: the sentinel guard, the hardened anchor and the named refusal in `quantlab/dataset/crsp.py`;
