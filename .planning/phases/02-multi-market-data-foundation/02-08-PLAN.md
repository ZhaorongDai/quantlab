It closes a genuine correctness gap the user identified after that work landed: without this, any historical backtest over "the S&P 500" or "the Nasdaq market" would silently suffer survivorship bias (using today's constituents/listings retroactively) and would have no data for stocks that have since delisted.

**Locked decisions resolved by this plan (research's open questions A1/A4 — do not leave ambiguous):**
- **A1 (xarray/Zarr boundary):** the universe/interval table is a reference/metadata artifact, NOT pipeline data flowing between `Dataset`/`Factor`/`Model` stages. - **A4 (OTC/exchange scope):** NASDAQ-listed common stock only (`exchange == "NASDAQ"`, `assetType == "Stock"`, `priceCurrency == "USD"`) — no OTC/Expert-Market tiers. Matches the objective's literal "Nasdaq market" framing; broader US-market coverage is out of scope for this supplement.
- **Ignore RESEARCH.md's "Alpaca" material entirely** — it resulted from a hallucinated interaction and must not be cited or acted on. Only the Tiingo/Wikipedia/datahub findings are used.

Purpose: enable correct, look-ahead-free historical backtesting universes in future phases (Phase 5/6), and complete delisted-stock coverage for US equities per the user's explicit correction.
