<assumption_delta_decision>
Detector: `assumption-delta scan 03.2 --json` returned `detected: true`, one signal,
`kind: pluralization`, term `second` ("a **second** vendor (Alpaca)").

**The noun that is now primary: the batched vendor request page — `(vendor, batch, page)`.**
Before this phase the unit of acquisition work was a *symbol*: `Acquisition._fetch_and_write(symbol,
start_date, end_date)` was the abstract primitive, a raw file was `raw/{symbol}/data.pqt`, and a
watermark was per symbol. After this phase the unit of work is one vendor request covering a *batch*
of symbols and returning one *page*, and the raw tier's identity gains a `vendor` segment.

**Decision: `promote`.** The generalized representation becomes primary and the specific one is
demoted to a detail of one variant:

- `_fetch_page(symbols, ...)` is promoted to the sole abstract fetch primitive. The single-symbol
  fetch is not kept alongside it — it is retired, and Tiingo expresses itself as the degenerate case
  via `DEFAULT_BATCH_SIZE = 1`. No transitional alias survives past the commit that lands the swap
  (03.1 D-03 precedent).
- `ConcurrentTiingoAcquisition` is demoted out of existence: the orchestration it owned is promoted
  to `Acquisition`, and the two tests naming the class directly are updated in the same commit as the
  collapse rather than being served by a permanent alias.
- The raw path's primary key gains `vendor`; the per-symbol directory is demoted from a structural
  segment to a hive partition *value* that only the `tick` frequency still uses.

This is not a re-litigation: SC-1's "no placeholder anywhere in the hierarchy" and D-01's
"single-symbol vendor is the degenerate case" are already a promote instruction from the developer.
What this block adds is the concrete naming of the promoted noun and the explicit refusal to
add-alongside.

**Companion contract test (suggested, implemented in 03.2-03-PLAN.md Task 3):** every concrete
`Acquisition` subclass round-trips a multi-symbol batch through the same base-class `_fetch_batch`
path, asserted by iterating `Acquisition.__subclasses__()` rather than by naming the two vendors.
That test goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces a per-symbol fetch primitive.
</assumption_delta_decision>
